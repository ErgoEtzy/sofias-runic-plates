# Amulets and magical objects

## In the book

Not every runic plate is automatically an amulet. The label fits best when the object could be worn and the inscription points to protection. *Magical object* is the broader category used for plates that were meant to heal, protect, compel, or harm.

[Book p. 30](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=32) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

An amulet is normally understood as a portable object believed to protect its bearer. *Apotropaic* is the more specific term for something intended to turn away danger or evil. These broader labels help explain the plates, but the inscription and find context still need to be considered separately.
