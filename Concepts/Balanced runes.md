# Balanced runes

## In the book

Often called mirror runes. The book uses *balanced runes* because the branches appear to be balanced around the stave, not simply flipped from an ordinary rune.

[Book p. 58](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=60) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

The term is specific to the book's analysis. In catalogues and older discussions, related forms may appear under labels such as mirror runes, reversed runes, or inverted runes. Those labels describe appearance; they do not settle how a sign was constructed or read.
