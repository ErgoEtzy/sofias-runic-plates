# Bind-runes

## In the book

A bind-rune combines two or more runes in one sign. It can be a simple correction, but it can also hide or complicate a reading. The shape alone does not always reveal which use was intended.

[Book p. 54](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=56) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Bind-runes occur in several periods and settings. They are a writing technique, not a single symbol system: some save space, some repair a carving, and some appear deliberately elaborate.
