# Conventional runes

## In the book

The familiar long-branch and short-twig forms. Of the 46 plates, 23 use only conventional runes and another 16 mix them with less usual forms.

[Book p. 292](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=294) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Both long-branch and short-twig runes belong to the sixteen-character Younger Futhark used during the Viking Age. The names describe graphic traditions rather than separate languages or neatly separated national alphabets.
