# Cryptic runes

## In the book

The book uses *cryptic runes* as an umbrella for forms that complicate an inscription: staveless, coordinate, balanced, and unconventional bind-runes, along with proposed older runes and runes carrying extra non-graphematic branches.

[Book p. 32](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=34) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

A cryptic form may still encode ordinary language. This matters because difficult-looking signs should not be treated as meaningless or magical before practical decoding options have been tested.
