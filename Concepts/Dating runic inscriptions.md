# Dating runic inscriptions

## In the book

The catalogue distinguishes archaeological dates from runological dates based on rune forms and language. For comparison, the plates are grouped into Early Viking Age (750-950), Mid Viking Age (950-1050), and Late Viking Age (1050-1150).

[Book p. 32](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=34) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

A runological date is usually a range rather than a precise year. Letter forms, spelling, language, ornament, associated finds, and archaeological layers may point in the same direction, but they do not always carry equal weight.
