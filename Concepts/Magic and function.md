# Magic and function

## In the book

Most readable plates appear to contain spells that tell someone or something to act. Ritual formulas and words meant to drive something away also occur. The inscriptions belong in a magical setting, although an unusual rune shape is not automatically a magical symbol.

[Book p. 307](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=309) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

The plate's function can involve several layers at once: the wording, the act of carving, spoken performance, how the object was carried or deposited, and the setting in which it was found. No single feature explains every plate.
