# Plate types

## In the book

The plates fall into a few practical groups. **A** has a hole and could be suspended, **B** has no hole, and **C** is folded or rolled. A damaged piece is called **fragmentary** when its original shape is unclear.

[Book p. 30](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=32) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

This is a working typology for the corpus, not a universal classification of runic objects. Shape can suggest how a plate was used, but a hole does not prove protection and a folded plate does not reveal its purpose by itself.
