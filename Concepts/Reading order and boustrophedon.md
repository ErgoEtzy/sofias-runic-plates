# Reading order and boustrophedon

## In the book

Reading order may be straightforward, reversed, spiral, or boustrophedon. In boustrophedon, each new line turns 180 degrees relative to the previous one, like the back-and-forth path of a plough.

[Book p. 32](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=34) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Direction is part of the reading, not just the layout. Reversing the order of signs or overlooking a turned row can change word divisions and produce an entirely different interpretation.
