# Rune-like markings

## In the book

Rune-like markings resemble parts of runes but do not form a stable sequence of writing signs. The term is kept separate from runiforms and rune-like signs when the marks may be scratches, ornament, damage, or another non-writing feature.

[Book p. 65](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=67) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Surface marks can be difficult to classify on corroded or worn metal. Repeated shape, alignment, carving technique, and relation to nearby signs all matter when deciding whether a mark belongs to an inscription.
