# Runes as writing and magical symbols

## In the book

The book treats the plates as magical objects, while the runes usually work as writing. Their force comes mainly from the words. A smaller number of signs may also have worked as symbols, but this is not presented as the normal use.

[Book p. 311](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=313) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Runes can represent sounds, words, or occasionally the concept carried by a rune-name. That range does not justify treating every rune as a magical symbol; the local text and material setting remain decisive.
