# Runes with non-graphematic branches

## In the book

Some rune shapes have extra branches that do not change the sound. The extra lines may hide the reading, add force to the sign, or serve as decoration. The first two possibilities are especially difficult to prove.

[Book p. 55](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=57) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

A graphematic feature helps distinguish one written sign from another. A non-graphematic branch is therefore an added stroke that does not create a different rune value. Its purpose has to be argued from the whole inscription rather than assumed from shape alone.
