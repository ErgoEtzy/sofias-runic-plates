# Runic plates

## In the book

Here, a *runic plate* is a small, flat metal object carrying runes or rune-like marks. Coins, fittings, and objects with an obvious everyday use are excluded. The Viking-Age catalogue contains 46 plates.

[Book p. 30](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=32) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Runic writing also survives on stone, wood, bone, horn, weapons, jewellery, and household objects. The metal plates form a narrow corpus because their shape, inscriptions, and possible magical use can be compared consistently.
