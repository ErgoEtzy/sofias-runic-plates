# Runic signatures and databases

## In the book

Each inscription has a signature, usually combining a regional or publication abbreviation with a number or reference. The notes preserve the signatures used in the Scandinavian Runic-text Database so that each plate can be matched with catalogues and later research.

[Book p. 29](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=31) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

A signum is an identifier, not a transcription. The Swedish National Heritage Board's Runor service combines text, normalization, translations, images, find information, and bibliography for thousands of inscriptions.
