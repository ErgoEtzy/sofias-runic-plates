# Runological method

## In the book

The method begins with the object: repeated examination under a stereomicroscope, movable lighting, photographs, drawings, and comparison with other inscriptions. A proposed interpretation should follow the reading rather than control it.

[Book p. 34](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=36) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

Runology combines epigraphy, historical linguistics, archaeology, and object study. Strong interpretations make each step visible: what is carved, how it is transliterated, how words are divided, which language forms are proposed, and where uncertainty remains.
