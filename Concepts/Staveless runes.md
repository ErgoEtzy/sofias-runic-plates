# Staveless runes

## In the book

Staveless runes are simplified forms, although the name is slightly misleading because many were not made by simply dropping the main stave. They have been proposed for Sigtuna plates 1 and 2, and both cases are reconsidered in the book.

[Book p. 53](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=55) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

They belong to the wider Younger Futhark tradition and are sometimes called Hälsinge runes. Their compressed shapes make correct orientation and comparison especially important.
