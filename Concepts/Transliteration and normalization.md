# Transliteration and normalization

## In the book

Transliteration replaces rune signs with Latin letters. Normalization goes a step further by presenting the words in a standardized historical language form, such as runic Swedish or runic Danish. Translation then gives the meaning in a modern language.

[Book p. 31](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=33) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

These layers answer different questions. Transliteration records the proposed signs; normalization makes forms easier to compare and look up; translation communicates meaning. None is a neutral copy of the previous layer.
