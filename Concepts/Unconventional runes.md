# Unconventional runes

## In the book

A broad label for cryptic runes, unusual shapes, and rune-like signs. Sixteen plates mix these with ordinary runes, while five use only unconventional forms. An unusual shape is not proof of magic by itself.

[Book p. 292](../Sofia_Pereswetoff_Morath_Viking_Age_Runic_Plates_Readings_and_Interpretations.pdf#page=294) / [book note](<../Sofia Pereswetoff-Morath - Viking-Age Runic Plates.md>)

## Wider context

The category is descriptive, not an alphabet. Each form still needs to be tested as a variant, correction, ligature, cipher, symbol, decoration, or non-writing mark.
