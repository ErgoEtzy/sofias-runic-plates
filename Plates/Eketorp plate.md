# Eketorp plate

A bronze runic plate from Eketorp Fort, Eketorp, Gräsgård parish, Öland. Sofia dates it to V. In the catalogue it is type fragmentary.

The inscription is still left open in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 37
- **SRD:** Öl ATA326-1087-2007
- **Other signature:** None listed
- **Found:** 1970, Eketorp Fort, Eketorp, Gräsgård parish, Öland
- **Context:** domestic (urban, fortification)
- **Object:** type fragmentary; bronze; 40–49 mm long, 13–15 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional runes (balanced runes, u bind-runes as on the Roskilde plate) Gamla (Old) Uppsala plate 1 Gamla (Old) Uppsala plate 2
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** from right to left on the A-side and straightforward on the B-side
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: SHM 31597. Sofia examined it 3 times.
