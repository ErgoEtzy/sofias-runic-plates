# Gorodišče plate 2

A bronze runic plate from Rjurikovo Gorodišče, Novgorod, Russia. Sofia dates it to 900s (eV), runological dating. In the catalogue it is type A.

The inscription is interpreted in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 11
- **SRD:** RU Melnikova2001;189
- **Other signature:** None listed
- **Found:** 1983, Rjurikovo Gorodišče, Novgorod, Russia
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment, fortification)
- **Object:** type A; bronze; 49 mm long, 12–13 mm wide, 1 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** unconventional
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: NHM: 1643/3. Sofia examined it once.
