# Gorodišče plate 2

Gorodišče plate 2 is a bronze plate found at Rjurikovo Gorodišče, Novgorod, Russia. It is listed as type A. The runes date it to 900s (eV).

The book includes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 11
- **SRD:** RU Melnikova2001;189

- **Context:** domestic (urban environment, fortification)
- **Object:** type A; bronze; 49 mm long, 12-13 mm wide, 1 mm thick
