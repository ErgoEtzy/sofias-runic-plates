# Gällungs plate 2

A bronze runic plate from Gällungs, Gotland. Sofia dates it to 900s (eV). In the catalogue it is type C, roll.

The inscription is still left open in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 14
- **SRD:** G 261
- **Other signature:** None listed
- **Found:** 1973, Gällungs, Gotland
- **Context:** grave
- **Object:** type C, roll; bronze; 45 mm long when straightened, 21 mm wide, 9 mm in diameter

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes)
- **Layout:** two sides, four rows on the outer side; the inside is impossible to see but appears to contain carved lines
- **Reading order:** undetermined
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: SHM 32391. Sofia examined it once.
