# Hässelby plate

Hässelby plate is a bronze plate found at Hässelby, Börje parish, Uppland. It is listed as type C, fragmentary, pendant. Archaeological evidence dates it to 1000s (mV).

No interpretation is given in the book.

- **Catalogue no.:** 22
