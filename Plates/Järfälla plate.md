# Järfälla plate

Järfälla plate is a copper plate found at Veddesta farm, Järfälla, Uppland. It is listed as type B. Archaeological evidence dates it to 1000 (mV).

The book includes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 23
- **SRD:** U Fv1969;210
- **Other signature:** —
- **Found:** 1962, Veddesta farm, Järfälla, Uppland
- **Context:** grave
- **Object:** type B; copper; 47 mm long, 22 mm wide, 0.20 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (long-branch runes), one bind-rune (correction)
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** a network of diagonal lines covering the entire inscription
