# Kvinneby plate

Kvinneby plate is a copper plate found at Södra Kvinneby, Stenåsa parish, Öland. It is listed as type A. The runes date it to 1000-1150 (lV).

The book includes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 40
- **SRD:** Öl SAS1989;43
- **Other signature:** none listed
- **Found:** 1955, Södra Kvinneby, Stenåsa parish, Öland
- **Context:** unknown
- **Object:** type A; copper; 52 mm long, 49 mm wide, 1.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (long-branch runes, dotted runes g, y, e) and unconventional (runes with non-graphematic branches)
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; five rows on the obverse and four on the reverse. A picture of a fish terminates the inscription.
- **Reading order:** boustrophedon
- **Punctuation:** a cross-shaped opening sign
- **Decoration:** depiction of a fish

- **Current location:** SHM 25654
