# Ladoga plate

A copper runic plate from Varangian Street, Staraja Ladoga, Russia. Sofia dates it to 800–950 (eV), archaeological and runological dating. In the catalogue it is type A (+bronze attachment).

The inscription is interpreted in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 12
- **SRD:** RU Melnikova2001;196
- **Other signature:** None listed
- **Found:** 1975, Varangian Street, Staraja Ladoga, Russia
- **Context:** domestic (urban)
- **Object:** type A (+bronze attachment); copper; 48 mm long, 14.2–18 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** unconventional (balanced runes, older runes) and one conventional rune (s).
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; two rows on each side
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: Staraja Ladoga Historical-Architectural and Archaeological Museum: САЕ-75. ЛП-1/1303. Sofia examined it 5 times.
