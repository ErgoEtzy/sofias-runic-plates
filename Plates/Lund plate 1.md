# Lund plate 1

Lund plate 1 is a most probably copper alloy plate found at Lund, Scania. It is listed as type A (+ metal plaque). Archaeological evidence dates it to 1000s (mV).

No interpretation is given in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 6
- **SRD:** DR Fv1993;226
- **Other signature:** DK no: SkL 74
- **Found:** 1990, Lund, Scania
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment)
- **Object:** type A (+ metal plaque); most probably copper alloy; 55 mm long, 20 mm wide, 4 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional
- **Layout:** runes are visible on the obverse, with lines terminating the row visible along the long side
- **Reading order:** from right to left and from left to right in the same row with a starting point in the centre of the writing surface
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** Lund Museum of Cultural History: 76420:172
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
