# Roskilde plate

A bronze runic plate from Roskilde. Sofia dates it to 1050–1100, (lV) runological dating. In the catalogue it is type A (+copper wire).

The inscription is interpreted in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 5
- **SRD:** DR 246
- **Other signature:** DK no: Sj 27
- **Found:** 1866, Roskilde
- **Context:** unknown
- **Object:** type A (+copper wire); bronze; 90 mm long, 10–12 mm wide, 1.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional and unconventional runes
- **Layout:** two sides, not divided into rows
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** a cross-shaped mark (x) of punctuation and a stroke (')
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: KNM: D 29. Sofia examined it once.
