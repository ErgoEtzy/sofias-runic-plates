# Sigtuna plate 4

Sigtuna plate 4 is a recorded as both copper and bronze plate found at the ‘Granhäcken’ block, Sigtuna, Uppland. It is listed as type fragmentary. Archaeological evidence dates it to 1000-1150 (lV).
