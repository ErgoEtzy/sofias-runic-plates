# Skørrebrovej plate

A bronze runic plate from Skørrebrovej, Bornholm. Sofia dates it to 1000–1150 (lV), runological dating. In the catalogue it is type fragmentary.

The inscription is interpreted in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 3
- **SRD:** DR DKBh63
- **Other signature:** DK no: Bh 63
- **Found:** 2005, Skørrebrovej, Bornholm
- **Context:** unknown
- **Object:** type fragmentary; bronze; 39 mm long, 15 mm wide, 0.5 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes, bind-runes)
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; two rows on each side
- **Reading order:** boustrophedon
- **Punctuation:** cross-shaped marks of punctuation
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: KNM: D 199/2006. Sofia examined it 2 times.
