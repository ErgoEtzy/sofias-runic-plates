# Solberga plate 2

A copper runic plate from Solberga 4:11, Köpingsvik, Öland. Sofia dates it to 1050–1150 (lV), archaeological and runological dating. In the catalogue it is type A, three holes.

The inscription is interpreted in the book.

## Quick facts

- **Sofia's catalogue:** no. 42
- **SRD:** Öl Fv1976;96B
- **Other signature:** None listed
- **Found:** 1972, Solberga 4:11, Köpingsvik, Öland
- **Context:** domestic (urban environment, workshop)
- **Object:** type A, three holes; copper; 84–85 mm long, 12–19 mm wide, 1 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional (long-branch runes, one dotted y-rune)
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; two rows on each side (no line dividers)
- **Reading order:** straightforward
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

Now held at: SHM find-ID: 1182776 (find no 1413; RAÄ no: 1533/74). Sofia examined it 3 times.
