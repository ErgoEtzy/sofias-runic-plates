# Østermarie plate

Østermarie plate is a silver plate found at Englyst, Bornholm. It is listed as type A, fragmentary. Archaeological and runological evidence dates it to 1000-1125 (lV).

The book includes an interpretation of the inscription.

## Quick facts

- **Catalogue no.:** 4
- **SRD:** DR AUD1999;288
- **Other signature:** DK no: Bh 58
- **Found:** 1998, Englyst, Bornholm
- **Context:** domestic (rural environment)
- **Object:** type A, fragmentary; silver; 22 mm long, 20 mm wide, 1 mm thick

## Runes and layout

- **Rune forms:** conventional runes (long-branch runes, dotted runes e and perhaps g)
- **Layout:** two sides, divided into rows; three rows on the obverse and four on the reverse
- **Reading order:** boustrophedon in spirals
- **Punctuation:** no
- **Decoration:** no

## Where it is now

- **Current location:** NM: D 10/2000
- **Examined by Pereswetoff-Morath:** once
